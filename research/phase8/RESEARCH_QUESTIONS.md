# Phase 8 Research Questions

## Primary question
Does the video-derived NIFTY monthly-options call-ladder-to-spread strategy generate statistically and economically robust positive net returns after realistic Indian market execution costs and margin requirements?

## Secondary questions
1. Does the premium formula for selecting the far OTM short call create a reproducible payoff advantage over ordinary bull call spreads?
2. Is the pre-lock structure's theta decay actually favorable after accounting for its short-gamma and short-tail exposure?
3. Does buying back the middle short call after a fixed decay interval improve risk-adjusted performance and materially reduce required margin?
4. Is the claimed margin relief reproducible across expiries, volatility regimes and brokers/exchange margin states?
5. Does the strategy outperform matched-risk controls after Paytm Money brokerage, STT, GST, exchange charges, bid/ask spread and slippage?
6. Which market regimes, if any, materially change its expectancy?
7. Is performance driven by genuine option-surface/payoff effects or by data artifacts, illiquidity, selection rules or favorable cost assumptions?
8. Does the strategy remain viable under adverse execution, wider spreads and realistic stress scenarios?
