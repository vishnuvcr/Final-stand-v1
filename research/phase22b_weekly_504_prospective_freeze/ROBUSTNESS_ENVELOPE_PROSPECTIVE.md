### Interpretation
This is encouraging descriptive evidence, but it is **not confirmatory**. Six weekly observations are insufficient for the frozen bootstrap gate (requires >=8), and far below the 30-cycle capital-promotion requirement. The zero drawdown is a property of this short six-cycle sample and must not be interpreted as an expected long-run drawdown.

## Prospective protocol
No K1/K2/K3 member will be selected. All 30 remain frozen. New qualifying weekly cycles will be evaluated for all 30 using the same entry, lock, stop, slippage and cost rules as the 504 control family.

## Decision rule
Do not promote capital from this sub-analysis. Confirmation requires additional chronological observations and the existing execution/capital evidence gates.
